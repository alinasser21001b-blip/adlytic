Blueprint gaps — 11

The instruction was: if implementation requires a product decision Blueprint v3 does not contain, stop and document the gap instead of inventing a solution. These are those gaps. Nothing below was invented.

These are not validation failures They are decisions the Blueprint does not contain. The technical architecture is internally consistent without them; several screens simply cannot be built until they are answered. Each gap names what it blocks.
BD-1 · Domain model blocker §2 ERD vs §4 M4
The gap
Saved pharmacies (screen M4) have no entity in the §2 ERD.
What is needed
Is a saved pharmacy per Account or per Subject? Is it capped? Does it survive branch closure?
What we are doing
No entity modelled. M4 cannot be built until this is answered.
BD-2 · Domain model blocker §2 ERD vs §4 V7/O22 · D31
The gap
Price disputes are a screen (V7), an operator queue (O22) and a decision (D31), with no entity in the ERD.
What is needed
What is a dispute's lifecycle? Can a branch respond? Does it resolve, or only accumulate? Does it affect the honoured rate, which D11 defines without it?
What we are doing
An event (price.dispute.raised) is modelled because D31 names the routing, but no persisted entity or state machine. O22 cannot be built.
BD-3 · Domain model blocker §2 ERD vs §4 M15/P30/O20/O21
The gap
Support tickets appear on four screens with no entity, no states and no ownership.
What is needed
Ticket lifecycle, who may read one, whether a patient ticket may reference a subject, and retention.
What we are doing
Not modelled. M15, P30, O20 and O21 cannot be built.
BD-4 · Domain model major §2 ERD vs §4 O10 · D29
The gap
Unmatched searches are a retention promise (D29) and an operator screen (O10) with no entity.
What is needed
Retention period, whether a term is linked to an account, and how it is aggregated.
What we are doing
Modelled as an event only (search.unmatched) with no persisted table, because D29 says the term is stored but not for how long or against what. O10 cannot be built and D29's retention promise is unverifiable until answered.
BD-5 · Identity blocker §4 O19
The gap
The consented support session has a screen and rules but no entity, no state machine and no consent-capture surface on the patient side.
What is needed
Which patient screen shows the consent prompt? How long is time-boxed? What does the banner appear on? What happens if the user is offline?
What we are doing
An endpoint stub returns awaitingConsent; no consent flow is modelled. O19 cannot be built.
BD-6 · Identity major §6 Grant machine vs §4 S9/P23
The gap
Claim invites and staff invites are states inside other machines with no entity of their own, so an invite cannot be listed, resent or cancelled.
What is needed
Can an invite be resent? Cancelled? How many are allowed? Does resending extend the 7-day expiry?
What we are doing
Modelled inside peer_grants and staff rows. Resend and cancel are unimplementable until answered.
BD-7 · Privacy major §4 M8/L02/P27
The gap
Export is an action with an artifact — a link with an expiry — and no entity anywhere.
What is needed
Format, link expiry, whether the artifact is retained, and whether an export appears in the access log.
What we are doing
Endpoints return an exportId; the artifact lifecycle is unmodelled.
BD-8 · Notifications major §4 E10/M6
The gap
Push requires a device registration entity that the ERD does not contain, and D35 removed the premium capabilities without stating the delivery fallback.
What is needed
One device or many per account? What happens when a token is invalidated? Is there a fallback when push is refused — the app is usable without it, but nothing says what replaces the alert.
What we are doing
notification-service is modelled with a device registration interface but no entity or table. Push delivery is unimplementable until answered; every notification-dependent flow degrades to in-app only.
BD-9 · Offline minor §6 Session & outbox vs §2 ERD
The gap
The outbox is a machine and two screens with no entity — correctly, since it is client-side only.
What is needed
None. Recorded so a reader does not mistake its absence from the ERD for an omission.
What we are doing
Modelled as client-local state in both apps. No server table. This gap is closed by explanation, not by a decision.
BD-10 · Marketplace major §8 routing rule 5 vs §3
The gap
Routing rule 5 excludes a branch that marked an item 'not carried', and P7 offers it as a decline reason — but the stock module was removed from Phase 0, so nothing persists the mark.
What is needed
Is 'not carried' a persistent branch-level fact, or only a one-off decline reason? If persistent, where does a branch un-mark it, given K9 was removed?
What we are doing
Routing rule 5 is documented and unimplementable. The decline reason is recorded on offer_lines only.
BD-11 · Marketplace minor §8 observed availability
The gap
Observed availability is defined as branches answering available 'in the last 7 days', with no rule for what a patient sees when the count is zero versus unknown.
What is needed
Does zero mean 'nobody has it' or 'nobody has been asked'? §13 of v1 insisted these are different; v3's §8 does not carry the distinction forward.
What we are doing
Modelled as a computed value; the display rule is left to the product decision.

Is the team ready to build?

Yes for the core loop; no for 4 screens. Everything in Blueprint v3's request → offer → reservation → collection path is fully specified here: entities, states with their producing paths, events with producers and consumers, endpoints with authorisation, tables with constraints, and tests that trace back to the Blueprint.

The blocked screens are BD-1, BD-2, BD-3, BD-5 — saved pharmacies, price disputes, support tickets and the consented support session. Each needs a product decision, and each is a decision an engineer would otherwise make by accident, which is exactly what this exercise exists to prevent.
